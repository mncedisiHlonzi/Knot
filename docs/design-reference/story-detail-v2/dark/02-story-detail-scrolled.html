How my grandmother counted the days…

Every market day she tied one more knot into a length of cotton cord, and when the week came back around she began to untie them, slowly, in the same order.

I was nine before I understood it was a calendar. She never called it that. She said the cord was how the days stayed in the house.

When she died, the cord was still on the nail by the door, with three knots left.
